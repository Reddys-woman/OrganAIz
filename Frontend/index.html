About OrganAIz

The story, the mission, and the humans behind it.

Why we built this

We take a screenshot, save a voice note, or download a PDF a dozen times a day — and lose track of most of it within the hour. OrganAIz exists because organizing that mess shouldn't be another chore on your list. It should just happen, quietly, in the background, so the only thing left for you to do is remember that you saved something — OrganAIz handles the rest.

We're a small team obsessed with making "where did I save that?" a question you never have to ask again.

Built by Code Catalyst

OrganAIz is designed and developed by Code Catalyst, a small team building focused, AI-native tools for everyday information overload.

Our Product

Organizing happens automatically. You just capture.

Smart Capture & Tagging

Drop in a screenshot or file and OrganAIz's AI reads it, writes a title and summary, and tags it — so you never have to name a file again.

Auto Collections

Related memories group themselves into collections by topic, so "that shopping list" and "that hackathon deadline" each end up exactly where you'd look for them.

Instant Recall Search

Search in plain language — "the hackathon deadline," "that receipt from last week" — and get the right memory back immediately.

Trash & Restore

Delete with confidence. Everything sits safely in Trash until you restore it or clear it for good — nothing disappears by accident.

Every Format Welcome

Images, voice recordings, PDFs, and documents all live in one place, each with its own dedicated view when you need to browse just one type.

Eye-Friendly Dark Mode

A genuinely muted dark theme, not just inverted colors — easy on the eyes for late-night capturing, and it remembers your choice everywhere.

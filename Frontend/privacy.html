Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This is a template Privacy Policy provided as a starting point for OrganAIz. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Please have it checked by a qualified legal professional for your jurisdiction — especially around data protection laws like GDPR or India's DPDP Act — before relying on it for a live product.

1. What We Collect

When you use OrganAIz, we may collect:

  • Content you upload — images, voice recordings, PDFs, and documents, along with the titles, summaries, tags, and collections our AI generates from them.
  • Account information — such as your name and email, once accounts are enabled.
  • Usage & preference data — like your dark/light mode preference, stored locally in your browser.

2. How We Use It

We use your information to:

  • Analyze and organize the Content you upload (titles, summaries, tags, collections).
  • Provide search, browsing, and Trash/Restore functionality.
  • Maintain and improve the Service.
  • Respond to support requests you send us.

3. AI Processing

Uploaded Content is sent to the Google Gemini API so it can be read and analyzed to generate titles, summaries, and tags automatically. Google's own privacy terms apply to how they process this data during analysis.

4. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your Content and its metadata are stored using Supabase, a third-party database and storage provider. We take reasonable steps to keep this data secure, but no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

OrganAIz uses your browser's local storage to remember simple preferences, like whether you've chosen dark or light mode. This stays on your device and isn't used for tracking or advertising.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

When you delete a memory, it moves to Trash, where it stays until you restore it or permanently delete it. Permanently deleted Content is removed from our systems and cannot be recovered. You can request full deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

7. Third-Party Service Providers

We share data only with the service providers needed to run OrganAIz — currently Google (Gemini API, for content analysis) and Supabase (data storage). We don't sell your data to advertisers or other third parties.

8. Children's Privacy

OrganAIz is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data? Reach us at support@organaiz.app.
